           
Humanitarian Commodity Dispatch Management Requirements
1. Overview
Humanitarian Commodity Dispatch is the process of planning, authorizing, dispatching, and tracking relief commodities from warehouses or hubs to designated Food Distribution Points (FDPs) located at kebele or woreda levels.
Commodity dispatch activities shall only be undertaken based on an approved Humanitarian Response Plan authorized by the Emergency Relief and Rehabilitation Directorate.
Managing
 Humanitarian Response Plans is outside the scope of this module. However, the system shall capture essential information related to 
approved response plans to support dispatch planning and execution.
2. Objectives
The Humanitarian Commodity Dispatch Module shall enable users to:
Register approved humanitarian response plans;
Assign sourcing hubs and warehouses to fulfill approved plans;
Prepare and authorize commodity dispatches;
Track dispatch execution against planned allocations;
Manage loading and issuance of commodities;
Generate dispatch documents and notifications; and
Maintain inventory and stockkeeping records.
3. Functional Requirements
3.1 Capture Approved Humanitarian Response Plans
The system shall allow users to register the details of approved humanitarian response plans.
Response Plan Information
Response Plan Reference Number
Approval Date
Response Type
Description
Food Distribution Point (FDP) Information
A response plan may contain one or more FDPs. For each FDP, the system shall capture:
FDP Name and Location
Number of Families to be Supported
Number of Beneficiaries
Expected Delivery Date
Commodity Requirements
A response plan may contain one or more commodity items. For each item, the system shall capture:
Commodity Item
Required Quantity
Unit of Measure
Associated FDP
3.2 Assign Sourcing Hubs and Warehouses
The system shall support allocation of approved response plans to one or more sourcing hubs or warehouses.
The system shall allow users to:
Select an approved humanitarian response plan;
Assign one or more hubs or warehouses to execute the response plan;
Allocate commodities and quantities to be dispatched from each hub or warehouse;
Associate allocations with specific FDPs;
Generate Dispatch Orders or Release Orders for each assigned hub or warehouse
 (includes destinations information)
; and
Notify the respective hubs or warehouses regarding their dispatch assignments.
The generated dispatch document shall authorize the movement of commodities from the assigned warehouse.
3.3 Dispatch Tracking and Monitoring
The system shall provide real-time visibility of dispatch execution against planned allocations.
The system shall allow users to:
View quantities dispatched from each hub or warehouse;
View quantities allocated but not yet dispatched;
Monitor dispatch status by commodity, warehouse, FDP, and response plan;
Monitor cumulative dispatch performance across all assigned hubs.
The system shall provide, at a minimum:
Per Hub/Warehouse
Quantity allocated for dispatch
Quantity dispatched
Quantity remaining to be dispatched
Per Response Plan
Total quantity allocated
Total quantity dispatched
Total quantity outstanding
3.4 Dispatch Authorization
When an approved vehicle arrives at a warehouse or hub, the warehouse manager or delegated officer shall verify the transportation authorization
 (transport order 
information )
 and prepare a dispatch authorization.
The system shall allow users to:
Select the relevant Dispatch Order;
Automatically retrieve commodity allocation details;
Record Transport Order Reference (optional);
Capture transporter information, including:
Transport company
Truck or trailer plate number
Driver name
Driver identification details
Identify the warehouse or stores from which commodities will be sourced;
Notify the assigned stores regarding the planned dispatch.
3.5 Commodity Location, Picking, and Loading
Upon arrival of the transporter at the assigned warehouse or store, commodities shall be located, picked, loaded, and prepared for dispatch.
The system shall support:
Commodity Picking
Identification of commodity location within the warehouse;
Picking commodities from specific stacks and inventory locations
 taking into account
;
Expiry dates; and
Receipt dates;
Generation of alerts for commodities nearing expiry
 (it may be deferred for latter)
.
Loading Management
Recording the number of bags, containers, or packages loaded onto the vehicle;
Capturing quantities confirmed by 
both
 storekeeper
 and driver
;
Requiring reconciliation of any discrepancies before dispatch documentation can be finalized.
3.6 Goods Issue Note (GIN) Preparation
Upon completion of loading, the system shall support preparation of Goods Issue Notes (GIN) and related dispatch documents.
The system shall:
Generate Goods Issue Notes (GIN);
Include transporter and waybill information;
Include references to the respective response plan and dispatch order;
Support verification and co-signature of dispatch documents;
 (Future plan)
Generate truck departure notifications to receiving locations and other concerned stakeholders, where applicable
 (This might also be deferred for latter)
.
3.7 Update Inventory and Stockkeeping Records
Upon completion of dispatch, the system shall automatically update inventory records.
The system shall update:
Bin Cards;
Stack Cards; and
Stock Ledgers.
All stock movements resulting from dispatch shall be fully traceable and auditable.
